UNIVERSITY OF MIAMI · Ph.D. RESEARCH

Selective Membranes for Olefin/Paraffin Separation

Olefin/paraffin mixtures are difficult to separate because the molecules have similar physical properties. My research explores polymer-based membranes and materials that can improve the balance between gas permeability and selectivity.

MEMBRANES • HYDROCARBONS • MATERIALS

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Polymer and mixed-matrix materials

Investigating polymer matrices and framework-based fillers as potential routes to selective, pressure-driven gas transport.

WHY IT MATTERS

Energy-efficient processes

Membrane systems may help reduce reliance on energy-intensive conventional separation processes.
